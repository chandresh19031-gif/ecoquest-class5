Teacher Guide

Designed for a teacher with one laptop + projector and a whole class.

📺 Classroom setup

  • Open EcoQuest on the classroom computer.
  • Use Classroom Mode for large-screen questions.
  • Read the question aloud and let students discuss before selecting.
  • Use “New Question” for another round.

🧑‍🏫 Suggested flow

  • 2 min: introduce the lesson.
  • 5 min: explore the key idea.
  • 8 min: play the classroom challenge.
  • 5 min: discuss why the answer is correct.
  • 2 min: connect the idea to daily life.

🌱 Learning principle

  • Prefer discussion over rapid clicking.
  • Ask “Why?” after every answer.
  • Use the activity prompts for observation or home/class tasks.
  • Keep the focus on understanding and practical action.

📖 Curriculum alignment

The source textbook describes Class 5 EVS as an integrated subject and emphasizes observation, discussion, activities, experiments, local examples, maps and charts. EcoQuest therefore uses interactive questions and teacher-led activities rather than treating the site as a replacement for the textbook.

INFORMATION • EDUCATION • COMMUNICATION

📚 From Textbook Learning to Visual Classroom Learning

The classroom challenge: students may struggle to understand concepts such as body parts, plants and other topics through text alone. EcoQuest therefore expands beyond digital games into IEC materials — charts, posters and classroom learning aids that can remain visible and useful throughout the school year.

🎯 Why IEC Materials?

The classroom needs identified for this initiative.

1

🧠 Learning Challenge

Students can find topics such as body parts, plants and other concepts difficult to understand through text alone. The goal is to move beyond memorisation toward clearer visual understanding.

2

👀 Need for Visual Learning

Textbooks may have limited pictorial representation. Engaging visual learning materials can provide additional ways for students to observe, connect and understand concepts.

3

📌 Proposed Solution

Develop IEC materials such as classroom charts, posters and learning aids that communicate important concepts in a simple, engaging and visual format.

4

🏫 Regular Classroom Usage

Materials can be displayed and used repeatedly in classrooms, giving students an opportunity to observe concepts while learning rather than relying only on one-time activities.

5

💬 Stakeholder Feedback

Discussions with multiple schools and educational institutions indicated a clear requirement for educational materials of this kind.

6

🖨️ Design Support Gap

Schools may have printing budgets but may lack the expertise, resources or mechanisms needed to design effective and engaging educational materials.

7

🤝 Our Contribution

EcoQuest supports schools by designing IEC materials aligned with the pedagogy and learning methodology used in classrooms.

8

📈 Evidence of Impact

The approach has already been tested in schools, and the activities have shown positive impact on students' learning and understanding.

9

📚 Curriculum Alignment

Materials are intended to be developed around the NCERT-aligned syllabus followed by participating schools.

10

🔄 Implementation

Identify relevant syllabus subtopics → convert them into engaging visual materials → develop a series of classroom activities and supporting resources.

11

📖 What IEC Means

IEC = Information, Education and Communication. The materials communicate information, support education and encourage meaningful classroom engagement.

🌱 EcoQuest's Classroom Philosophy

See it → Understand it → Discuss it → Do it → Remember it.

The website is therefore not positioned as a replacement for the textbook. It acts as a classroom companion combining visual IEC materials, teacher-led activities, interactive games, supporting videos and printable resources around the school's curriculum.
